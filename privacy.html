TranscribePress – Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. TranscribePress is made by NTQ37.

Your recordings and transcripts stay on your computer

The speech recognition runs on your own computer. Your audio and video files, transcripts, speaker names and remembered voices are stored only in this app on your device. They are never uploaded to NTQ37 or anyone else.

What is downloaded

The first time you transcribe, the app downloads the open-source AI models (Whisper, WeSpeaker) and the speech-engine library from Hugging Face and jsDelivr. These downloads contain no information about you or your files. After that the app works offline.

Microphone and screen

The microphone and screen-sharing (for meeting sound) are used only while you record, after you allow it. The recording is saved on your computer only.

Purchases

TranscribePress Pro is bought through the Microsoft Store. NTQ37 does not receive your payment details; the app only asks the Store whether Pro is owned.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.
