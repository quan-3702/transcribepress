How to use TranscribePress

1. Add a recording

Click ＋ Add audio or video or drop files on the window. MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, FLAC, MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM and more work. Choose the spoken language and the quality first (☰ → Transcription settings). Most accurate needs a graphics card; it makes the fewest mistakes with accents and languages other than English.

2. Read, listen and fix

  • Click a time to play from that line. The line being spoken is highlighted.
  • Click any word to change it. An empty line is removed.
  • 🧹 Remove “um” deletes filler words. 🔎 Find & replace fixes a name everywhere.

3. Who said what (Pro)

👥 Find speakers tells the voices apart. Type the names on the right (▶ plays each person) and press 🧠 Remember these voices – next time they are recognised by themselves. If two groups are the same person, choose “Same as…”.

4. Export

⬇ Export: Text and subtitles (SRT, VTT) are free. With Pro: Word, PDF, Markdown, web page, CSV and JSON – and 🔒 Hide private details replaces e-mails, phone numbers, card numbers, links and names you choose.

5. More Pro tools

  • 🔗 Share file: one HTML file with the sound inside. Anyone can open it in a browser and click a sentence to hear it.
  • ✂ Save a clip: click the first line, Shift + click the last line, save that part of the recording as a WAV file.
  • 🎙 Record → Meeting: records your microphone and the computer sound (Zoom, Teams, Meet…). Tick “Share system audio” or “Share tab audio”.
  • 📁 Watch a folder: every new recording you put in the folder is written down by itself while the app is open.
  • Many files at once, recordings of any length, translation into English.

Windows and Mac

On Windows install TranscribePress from the Microsoft Store. On a Mac (or Linux, Chromebook) open the app in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and choose Install in the address bar.
